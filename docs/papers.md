## About the conference paper

The paper proposes Adaptive Quantile Flexibility (AQF), a way to estimate how much of an aggregate
load profile is flexible by computing the quantile that separates it rather than hand-picking one,
and tests it on synthetic data. AQF is a separate study and is not part of the `pyntlp` package;
[AQF](aqf.md) summarises it, and its code, data and figures are in
[`publication/1_conference/`](https://github.com/mssamhan31/PyNTLP/tree/main/publication/1_conference).

## About the journal article

The article studies the Solar Sharer Offer with smart-meter data from Ausgrid's network. Nothing
from it is documented here before it is published.
